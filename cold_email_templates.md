## Tips for higher response rates

**Personalise the subject line**
Using the business name in the subject line gets significantly higher open rates than a generic subject.

**Send Tuesday to Thursday**
Monday mornings and Friday afternoons have lower open rates. Mid-week emails perform best.

**Send in the morning**
Between 8am and 10am. Business owners check email when they open the shop.

**Track who you have emailed**
In your shared Google Sheet, add a column "Emailed" with the date. Add "Email 2 sent" and "Email 3 sent" columns. This prevents you or your partner from emailing the same person twice.

**Reply quickly**
When someone responds, reply within the hour. Interest fades fast. Have your download link and pricing ready to send immediately.
